Three complete design languages applied to the app's real screens (home, practice question, pack list). Same content, three personalities. Pick one, mix elements, or reject all three.

Tangram Toybox

Candy-bright toy shelf. Chunky press-down buttons with hard offset shadows, thick outlines, sticker-like cards on warm cream. Tangram shapes are the brand motif (a Chinese puzzle made of math shapes — the only motif that owns both subjects). Duolingo-energy without imitating Duolingo. Type: Baloo 2 + ZCOOL KuaiLe for hanzi. Loudest of the three; pure kid joy.

Paper Lantern

Warm rice-paper canvas with a folk palette pulled from Chinese paper-cut art: vermillion, jade, gold. Lantern and seal motifs, hanzi set in ZCOOL KuaiLe as a first-class visual element rather than an afterthought. Calmest and most premium of the three — the one parents screenshot. Culturally rooted without being a costume.

Lantern Playground

Paper Lantern's folk palette taken to the playground: a bouncing panda guide, sunrise sky over jade hills, chunky press-down buttons with chocolate outlines, gold-underlined hanzi as the hero, a lantern riding the progress bar. Every answer tile carries a different folk color on its press edge. Warm AND loud — the culture and the joy at the same time.

Starlight Arcade

Learning adventure at night: deep indigo sky, glassy cards, glowing candy accents, star confetti. Feels like a game console the moment it opens — strongest "one more round" pull, gentlest on evening eyes, and the most differentiated (almost no kids' learning app dares a dark default). Type: Nunito Black + ZCOOL KuaiLe.

Sunrise Playground

The synthesis: B2's warm lantern world in full daylight, A's candy brightness, C2's no-black press physics (every chunky edge is a deeper shade of its own color), and the colorblind-safe palette — coral Chinese vs ocean-blue Math (survives all three CVD types), teal-mint success with a ✓ badge, gold reserved for delight only. Headings in warm brown, never black.
