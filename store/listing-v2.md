## Description

Mandarineer gives kids their first Chinese words. Your child hears a word from a native Mandarin speaker, taps the picture that matches, and hears the word again. Eight questions later, the session ends with stars and a cheer.

Built by a parent for their own child, then shared with every family.

WHAT YOUR CHILD DOES
• Hears a word in Mandarin and taps the matching picture
• Reads characters with pinyin underneath
• Builds sentences tile by tile (这是一只猫!)
• Counts and adds in a short math mode
• Collects stickers and hearts, one 8-question session at a time

WHEN AN ANSWER IS WRONG
Nothing bad happens. The word comes back later for another try. A review schedule brings each word back right before your child would forget it. No timers, no ads.

WHAT YOU SEE AS A PARENT
• A report for each child: words learned, words still wobbly, and what to practice together
• A row of the days your child played this week. A missed day leaves a gap. Nothing resets.
• A small mission after each session, like saying one Chinese word at dinner

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 33 packs and 360 words: animals, food, colors, numbers, family, weather, vehicles, jobs and more
• A native-speaker recording and a picture for every word
• Sentence practice with measure words and question patterns
• Math from single digits to 100

SAFE FOR KIDS
• One grown-up account, with a profile for each child
• A child's profile is a nickname and an animal buddy. No child email, no photos.
• Parent Zone sits behind a grown-ups question
• Progress syncs across iPhone, iPad and the web
• Packs your child has played work offline

PRICE
Some packs are free, with no time limit. One purchase opens every pack for every child on your account, on iPhone, iPad and the web. No subscription.
