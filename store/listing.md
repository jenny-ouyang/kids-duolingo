## Description
Mandarineer gives kids ages 4–8 their first Chinese words — spoken by real Mandarin voices, matched with friendly pictures, and practiced in short sessions that end in a celebration every time.

Built by a parent for their own child, then shared with everyone.

WHAT KIDS DO
• Hear a word in Mandarin, then tap the picture that matches
• Read simple characters with pinyin help
• Build real sentences tile by tile (这是一只猫!)
• Count and add with a gentle math mode
• Earn hearts and streaks, one 8-question session at a time

WHAT MAKES IT KIND
• Wrong answers are never punished — the word simply comes back for another try
• A spaced-repetition brain schedules each child's reviews right before they'd forget
• No ads, no timers, no pressure

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 33 word packs: animals, food, colors, numbers, family, weather, vehicles, jobs, and more
• 360 words, each with native-speaker audio and a friendly illustration
• Sentence practice with measure words and question patterns
• Early math: addition from single digits to 100
• Progress reports for parents: what's mastered, what's wobbly, what to practice together

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• One grown-up account, profiles for each child
• Progress syncs between iPhone, iPad, and the web
• A child's profile is just a nickname and an animal buddy — no child email, no photos, nothing personal
• Works offline once a pack has been played

Free to play. 加油!

## Review notes
Mandarineer is a children's Mandarin vocabulary app. No account is needed to review: the app signs in silently as a guest on first launch. Tap "Let's go!" after picking a buddy and a name, then tap any pack to practice.

Native functionality beyond the website (guideline 4.2):
1. All 484 Mandarin audio clips and all 360 word illustrations are bundled in the app and play with no network connection.
2. Offline practice: once a pack has been played, its questions are cached on the device. To verify, play one pack, turn on Airplane Mode, and replay the same pack.
3. Haptic feedback on correct and incorrect answers and on the end-of-session celebration.
4. Native splash screen and status bar.

The Parent Zone sits behind a grown-ups-only multiplication gate (any adult can pass it). Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Parent Zone → Account → "Delete account" → type DELETE. This removes the account, every child profile, and all progress immediately.

No third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. A child profile holds only a nickname and an emoji avatar.
